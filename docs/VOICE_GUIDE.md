# Advanced Solutions Direct: Voice & Copy Guide

Use this for the website, ads, emails, and call scripts so everything sounds like one company.

## Who we're talking to

People who feel overwhelmed by personal debt: credit cards, medical bills, personal loans, collection
calls. They're stressed, often embarrassed, and don't know who to trust. Many are reading on a phone,
late at night.

## Our big idea

**The first call when debt gets overwhelming.**

We are the calm, honest first step. A real person listens, explains how the process works, and, if
it's a fit, connects the person with an independent consumer rights law firm that may be able to help.

Every page should reinforce three promises we can actually keep:

1. **A real person.** You talk to someone who listens first and never judges.
2. **Straight answers.** Plain English about how this works. No jargon, no hype.
3. **You decide.** Free to talk, free to walk away. Nothing happens without your say.

## How we sound

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Calm and reassuring | Urgent or alarmist |
| Warm and human | Salesy or slick |
| Plain and specific | Vague or full of jargon |
| Honest about what we do and don't do | Over-promising |
| Respectful | Judgmental or pitying |

**Write like this:** short sentences, one idea each. Talk about the reader ("you") more than about
us. Name real situations people recognize. End sections with an easy next step.

## Words we use

- "Talk to a real person," "free call," "no pressure," "no obligation"
- "Straight answers," "how the process works," "your next step"
- "If it's a fit," "may be able to help"
- "Independent partners" (general) and "an independent consumer rights law firm" (when describing who
  does the legal work)
- "You decide"

## Words and claims we never use

These are compliance rules, not style preferences.

- **Never imply we do the work.** No "our attorneys," "we validate," "we negotiate," "we dispute,"
  "we'll stop the calls," "our program."
- **Never promise outcomes.** No savings percentages, "debt free," "eliminate," "erase," timelines
  ("in 24 months"), guaranteed results, or credit score claims.
- **Never mention stopping or pausing payments,** in either direction. That is between the client and
  the law firm.
- **No unverifiable proof.** No made-up stats, ratings, "thousands helped," "#1," "trusted by,"
  testimonials, or media logos unless they are real and approved.
- **No fake urgency.** No "act now," "limited time," countdowns, or "agents standing by."
- **Avoid "licensed"** unless counsel confirms it for every state we serve.
- Keep program details (fees, length, results) off marketing materials. The law firm explains those
  in writing.

## Calls to action

Primary: **"Get My Free Consultation"**, **"Call Now"**, **"Talk to a Real Person"**

Always pair a call to action with the low-risk reminder: free, no pressure, no obligation.

## Quick examples

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| We'll eliminate your debt. | Talk to a real person first. |
| Our attorneys will stop the calls. | If you hire an attorney, collectors generally have to contact the attorney instead of you. |
| Save up to 50%! | (Don't. No savings claims.) |
| Act now before it's too late! | Ready when you are. |
| Our debt relief program | The law firm explains what it can do for your accounts. |

## Before anything goes live

Check every line against the "never" list above, and have compliance review new claims.
